<Solution>

Vi sætter den ene `while`-løkke inde i den anden: den ydre går stien igennem, den indre fylder hvert hul helt op.

```python.run requires="codingpirates-maze"
from maze import Puzzle, Farmer

maze = Puzzle.from_file("courseD_farmer_while8_2025")
farmer: Farmer = maze.player

for i in range(6):
    while farmer.path_ahead():
        farmer.forward()
    while farmer.at_hole():
        farmer.fill()
    farmer.right()

Puzzle.done()
```

</Solution>
