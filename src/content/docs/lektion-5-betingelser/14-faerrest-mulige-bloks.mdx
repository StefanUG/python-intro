<Solution>

I stedet for at skrive `farmer.remove()` mange gange, bruger vi en `for`-løkke, der gentager det det rigtige antal gange.

```python.run requires="codingpirates-maze"
from maze import Puzzle, Farmer

maze = Puzzle.from_file("courseD_farmer_while3_2025")
farmer: Farmer = maze.player

farmer.forward()
for i in range(10):
    farmer.remove()

Puzzle.done()
```

</Solution>
