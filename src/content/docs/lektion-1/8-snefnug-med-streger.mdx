<Solution>

For at lave 10 ligeligt fordelte streger i en cirkel med 360 grader, skal man dreje 36 grader hver gang.

```python.run
from turtle import *

for i in range(10):
  forward(100)
  back(100)
  right(36)

done()
```
</Solution>
