<Solution>

To nye funktioner deler arbejdet: `lettuce_stairs()` tager sig af salaten i et trappemønster, og `corn_rectangle()` følger stien rundt i et rektangel og samler majs undervejs.

```python.run requires="codingpirates-maze"
from maze import Puzzle, Harvester

maze = Puzzle.from_file("courseE_farmer_functions13_2024")
farmer: Harvester = maze.player

def lettuce_stairs():
    for i in range(2):
        farmer.forward()
        farmer.right()
        farmer.forward()
        farmer.left()
        while farmer.has_lettuce():
            farmer.pick_lettuce()

def corn_rectangle():
    for i in range(4):
        while farmer.path_ahead():
            farmer.forward()
            if farmer.has_corn():
                farmer.pick_corn()
        farmer.left()

lettuce_stairs()
corn_rectangle()
lettuce_stairs()

Puzzle.done()
```

</Solution>
