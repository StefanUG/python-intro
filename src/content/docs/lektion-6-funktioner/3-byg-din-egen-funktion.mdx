<Solution>

Vi skriver selv, hvad `get_5()` skal gøre - en løkke, der tager 5 nektar - og kalder den, hver gang vi når en blomst.

```python.run requires="codingpirates-maze"
from maze import Puzzle, BeePlayer

maze = Puzzle.from_file("courseE_bee_functions3")
bee: BeePlayer = maze.player

def get_5():
    for i in range(5):
        bee.get_nectar()

bee.forward()
bee.right()
for i in range(2):
    get_5()
    bee.forward()
bee.left()
get_5()

Puzzle.done()
```

</Solution>
