<Solution>

Funktionen `move_and_collect()` drejer, samler al nektaren fra en blomst, flyver videre til en honningtavle og laver al honningen der.

```python.run requires="codingpirates-maze"
from maze import Puzzle, BeePlayer

maze = Puzzle.from_file("courseE_bee_functions_challenge2")
bee: BeePlayer = maze.player

def move_and_collect():
    bee.left()
    bee.forward()
    bee.forward()
    for i in range(6):
        bee.get_nectar()
    for i in range(2):
        bee.forward()
        for i in range(3):
            bee.make_honey()
    bee.forward()

for i in range(3):
    move_and_collect()
bee.right()
bee.forward()
bee.right()
bee.right()
move_and_collect()

Puzzle.done()
```

</Solution>
