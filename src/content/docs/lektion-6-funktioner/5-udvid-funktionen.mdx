<Solution>

```python.run requires="codingpirates-maze"
from maze import Puzzle, BeePlayer

maze = Puzzle.from_file("courseE_bee_functions5")
bee: BeePlayer = maze.player

def move_and_get_4():
    bee.right()
    bee.forward()
    for i in range(4):
        bee.get_nectar()
    bee.backward()
    bee.left()

bee.forward()
move_and_get_4()
bee.forward()
for i in range(2):
    bee.forward()
    move_and_get_4()

Puzzle.done()
```
