```python.run
# Gentag 10 gange
for i in range(2):
    forward(100)
    right(60)
    forward(100)
    right(120)
right(???)

done()
```

<Solution>

For 10 gentagelser i en hel cirkel skal vi dreje 36 grader hver gang.

```python.run
from turtle import *

for i in range(10):
    for j in range(2):
        forward(100)
        right(60)
        forward(100)
        right(120)
    right(36)

done()
```

</Solution>
